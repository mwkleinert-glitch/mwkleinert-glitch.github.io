TankDesk Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

TankDesk is a field-operations app for propane and tank-desk work. It is published by Micah Kleinert (Ellsworth Farmers Exchange / Ellsworth Energy context; Individual Apple Developer team).

Contact: mwkleinert@gmail.com

What this app does

TankDesk helps a small yard run the field day: role sign-in on the device, driver-day books, maps handoff, customer cards, and install photos. Version 1.0.0 ships with demo / fictional northern Michigan data. Live customer SMS is off. TankDesk does not write to Energy / AgVantage.

Data we collect

TankDesk does not operate a TankDesk account server for this version. Information you enter stays on the device unless you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet, Apple Maps, or Google Maps.

  • Location (optional). Used only when you open Maps for a stop. TankDesk does not run background GPS tracking in this version.
  • Photos and camera (optional). Used only if you attach a yard or tank photo to a card or packet.
  • Customer and route notes you type. Names, phones, addresses, and stop notes you enter are stored locally on the device for app functionality.
  • Role picker. Choosing Dispatcher, Manager, or a demo driver is local sign-in. There is no password account in this version.

What we do not do

  • No advertising network, no tracking across other companies’ apps, no sale of personal data.
  • No live SMS provider calls in the store binary (SMS stays mock).
  • No Energy / billing-system write-back.
  • No analytics SDK added for this 1.0.0 listing.

Maps

When you start turn-by-turn, Apple Maps or Google Maps opens as a separate app. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Children

TankDesk is built for adult field workers. It is not directed at children under 13.

Retention and deletion

Delete the app to remove local TankDesk data on that device. If you emailed support a screenshot or note, we keep it only long enough to answer you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
