First time in TankDesk

Read this after the icon is already on your phone. Live off. Energy still bills.

Open today’s book

1 Open the app

Tap the TankDesk icon on your Home Screen. You should see the navy header TankDesk Driver Day and a row of truck numbers.

If you see a wall of code, you are not in Safari. Close it. Open the Safari app. Go to mwkleinert-glitch.github.io

2 Pick YOUR truck

Tap your number once. It turns navy.

Wrong truck = wrong towns. If the name under the header is not you, tap again.

3 Pick the DAY you are running

Night before: tap tomorrow. Morning: tap today. Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri.

The line under the buttons should read your name, that day, stops 1–n, and road miles.

4 Read the book before you roll

Scroll the numbered list. Stop 1 is first. Last stop is last. After the last stop you go to the fill plant printed on the line (Alanson, Ellsworth, Kalkaska, or Boyne).

YELLOW STAY = regular on your truck. You will see them again.

BLUE JOIN = on your run today only. Still fill it. Don’t assume it is yours next week.

5 Start turn-by-turn

Tap the black button Start Apple Maps · turn-by-turn (or the blue Google button if that is what you use).

Apple Maps / Google Maps opens and talks. That is numbered leg 1.

Long days are split. When Maps says you arrived at the last pin of that leg, come back to TankDesk and tap Apple Maps · numbered leg 2.

6 At a stop

Fill the tank like you always do. Then either let Maps continue, or tap Apple next on that stop to go only to the next number.

Do not skip around the list unless Megan told you to cut a stop.

7 End of day

After the last numbered stop, Maps should send you to the closest fill. Pump. You are done with TankDesk for that book.

You know it is working when

  • Header shows your truck number and your name.
  • Day chip is the day you are running.
  • List starts at Stop 1 and counts up with no missing numbers.
  • Stops are yellow or blue, not all one color by accident of a bad tap.
  • Apple or Google opens with a driving route, not a dropped pin only.
  • The next house Maps names matches Stop 1 (or the next number you tapped).

Stop and call Megan / Micah if

  • The name is another driver.
  • The towns are not your territory.
  • Maps will not open.
  • Energy’s paper book and this list do not match and nobody told you which one wins today.

Energy still owns the money file. If the two lists fight, ask. Do not guess.
